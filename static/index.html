Comment RadarWho's really in the comments?

Find the accounts that act together.

Paste a YouTube video. Comment Radar reads its comments and the commenters' public profiles, finds groups of accounts that move as one, and has an Investigator and a Defense argue each case before it reports. It flags groups with evidence, never single people.

For
Journalists and fact-checkers who need to know whether a comment section's "public opinion" is real.
Looks at
Near-copy comments, bursts, young accounts replying together, accounts created in the same weeks, and sentiment.
Can't
See IP addresses or private data, read other platforms yet, or prove who runs an account.
